Casebook

Turns your surgical logbook export into charts you can filter, and shows the exact entries behind every number.

Open Casebook Source code and notes on GitHub

Before you use it

Try the made-up example first, and keep real logbook exports out of screenshots and bug reports.

Your file is read in your browser and never uploaded or saved. Reload the page and it’s gone.

It works with the standard spreadsheet you download from eLogbook, up to about 5 MB. Anything else is turned away rather than guessed at.

What it won’t do

One row is one logged procedure, not one patient, one theatre case or a measure of competence. It’s for exploring your logbook, not a validated clinical or portfolio assessment.
